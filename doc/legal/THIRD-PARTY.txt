AdaIN / pytorch-AdaIN
PyTorch implementation: Naoto Inoue and contributors.
Source: https://github.com/naoto0804/pytorch-AdaIN
Implementation license: licenses/pytorch-AdaIN.LICENSE.txt (MIT).
Upstream pretrained-model release:

Original AdaIN work: https://github.com/xunhuang1995/AdaIN-style

byte-for-byte.
